How long should a title tag be?

+

Google truncates titles by pixel width, roughly 580 px on desktop — usually 50–60 characters. Put the most important words first.

Does Google always use my meta description?

+

Not always. Google may rewrite snippets to match the search query, but a clear, relevant description is used more often and improves click-through rate.
