FAQ

Questions we hear about Web, Apps & SaaS.

Do you build websites or only complex software?

+

Both. We build company websites and landing pages as well as web applications, mobile apps, SaaS platforms and e-commerce systems.

Who owns the code?

+

You do. Source code, documentation and infrastructure access are handed over — there is no artificial lock-in.

Can you take over an existing project?

+

Yes. We start with a technical review of the codebase and infrastructure, then propose a plan to stabilise, improve or rebuild where it makes sense.
