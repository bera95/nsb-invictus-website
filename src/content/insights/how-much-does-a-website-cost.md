**Short answer:** the cost of a business website depends mainly on *scope* — the number of unique page templates, custom design, integrations, content, languages and how much SEO, performance and security work is included. A simple, well-built company site is a modest investment; a custom platform with integrations is a software project.

## What actually drives the price

1. **Unique templates, not page count.** Ten service pages sharing one template cost far less than ten different layouts.
2. **Design.** Adapting a theme is faster than a custom design system with interactions and animation.
3. **Integrations.** CRM, booking, payments, ERP, multilingual content and custom forms each add work.
4. **Content.** Copywriting, photography, video and translations are often underestimated.
5. **Search readiness.** Technical SEO, structured data, AEO/GEO preparation and analytics setup.
6. **Quality and security.** Testing, accessibility, performance optimisation, security hardening and documentation.

## Typical types of websites

- **Starter site** — a few pages, template-based, basic contact form. Best for very small businesses validating an offer.
- **Professional company website** — custom design, 10–30 pages, SEO foundations, analytics with consent, fast and secure hosting.
- **Advanced website / platform** — integrations, member areas, multilingual content, interactive tools or e-commerce.
- **Web application** — custom logic, user accounts, dashboards and workflows; priced as software development.

Exact numbers depend on your market and requirements, which is why a short discovery call saves money: it prevents paying for features you do not need.

## Ongoing costs to budget for

- Hosting and domain
- Security updates, monitoring and backups
- Content updates and new pages
- Analytics, consent management and reporting
- SEO, content marketing or advertising to bring visitors

## How to get the most value

- **Start with goals** — leads, sales, applications or trust — and design every page for that goal.
- **Prioritise speed and clarity** over decoration; they affect both conversions and rankings.
- **Own your code and accounts** — domain, hosting, analytics and source code should be in your name.
- **Measure from day one** with GA4 and Search Console so you can improve based on data.
