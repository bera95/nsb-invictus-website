**Short answer:** SEO helps you rank in Google’s list of results, AEO helps you *become the answer* in featured snippets, “People also ask” and voice search, and GEO helps AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot *mention and cite* your business. They share the same foundation, so the most efficient strategy treats them as one programme.

## SEO — search engine optimisation

SEO is the practice of making your pages rank for the searches your customers make. It rests on three pillars:

- **Technical health** — fast pages, clean indexation, mobile usability, structured site architecture.
- **Relevant content** — pages that match search intent better than competing pages.
- **Authority** — links, mentions and signals that your site is trustworthy.

If your site is not crawlable or indexable, nothing else on this page matters.

## AEO — answer engine optimisation

AEO focuses on the *answer formats* search engines now show above or instead of classic links: featured snippets, “People also ask”, knowledge panels, voice answers and AI overviews. To win them:

1. **Answer first.** Put a direct, two-to-three sentence answer under each question-style heading.
2. **Use question headings** that mirror real queries (“How much does…”, “What is…”).
3. **Add FAQPage and other schema.org markup** so the meaning of your content is explicit.
4. **Use lists and tables** for steps, comparisons and prices — they are easy to extract.

## GEO — generative engine optimisation

GEO is about visibility inside AI-generated answers. Large language models summarise the web, so you want your brand to be *one of the sources they trust and cite*. GEO work typically includes:

- **Crawler access** — allow AI *search* crawlers (for example OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot) in robots.txt, even if you block training crawlers.
- **Clear entity information** — consistent company name, services, location and contact details, marked up with Organization schema.
- **Citable content** — original explanations, definitions, comparisons and data that a model can quote.
- **Mentions beyond your site** — reviews, directories, articles and profiles that confirm who you are.
- **An llms.txt file** that points AI systems to your most important pages.

## How the three fit together

| Aspect | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goal | Rank in results | Be the direct answer | Be cited by AI assistants |
| Key levers | Technical, content, links | Answer-first structure, schema | Crawler access, entity clarity, citable content |
| Main measurement | Rankings, organic traffic | Snippets, impressions | AI-answer mentions, referral traffic from AI tools |

In practice, one well-built page serves all three: technically sound (SEO), structured with clear answers (AEO) and rich in specific, trustworthy information (GEO).

## A practical 30-day starting plan

1. **Week 1:** run a technical audit and fix indexation, speed and mobile issues.
2. **Week 2:** rewrite your top five service pages answer-first, with FAQs and schema.
3. **Week 3:** check AI crawler access, add Organization schema and an llms.txt file.
4. **Week 4:** publish two in-depth articles that answer your customers’ most common questions.
