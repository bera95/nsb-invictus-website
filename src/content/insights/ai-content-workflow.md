**Short answer:** AI can multiply content output, but only a *workflow* makes it rank and convert: research real customer questions, brief each piece, let AI draft, have an expert add experience and facts, optimise for search and AI answers, then publish and measure. Volume without that workflow produces generic text that neither readers nor search engines value.

## Step 1 — Research what people actually ask

Collect questions from sales calls, support tickets, Search Console queries, “People also ask” and community forums. Group them by topic and buying stage.

## Step 2 — Write a brief for every piece

A good brief includes the target reader, the main question, the angle, key points, internal links, sources and the call to action. AI drafts are only as good as their brief.

## Step 3 — Draft with AI, then add what AI cannot

AI produces a solid structure quickly. A subject-matter expert then adds:

- **First-hand experience** — real examples, lessons and opinions
- **Accurate facts** — checked numbers, current rules, correct terminology
- **Brand voice** — tone, vocabulary and positioning

## Step 4 — Optimise for search and AI answers

- Answer the main question in the first two sentences.
- Use question-style headings and short paragraphs.
- Add tables, steps and an FAQ section with schema markup.
- Link to related services, tools and articles.

Check length, reading time and readability with our [free word & character counter](/tools/word-counter/).

## Step 5 — Repurpose for social media

One strong article becomes a LinkedIn post, a carousel, a short video script and a newsletter section. AI helps adapt the text and create matching visuals in your brand style — a person approves every post.

## Step 6 — Measure and refresh

Track rankings, AI-answer mentions, traffic and leads per piece. Update articles that are close to page one, and retire content that no longer helps.

## Pitfalls to avoid

- Publishing unedited AI drafts
- Writing for keywords instead of questions
- Inconsistent visuals on social media
- No measurement, so nothing improves
