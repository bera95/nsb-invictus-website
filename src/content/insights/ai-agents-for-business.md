**Short answer:** an AI agent is software that uses a language model to *complete tasks*, not just answer questions. Connected to your tools — email, CRM, CMS, analytics, accounting — agents can draft, sort, research, report and update records around the clock, while your team approves what matters.

## Agents vs chatbots vs automation

- **Chatbot:** answers questions in a chat window.
- **Classic automation:** follows fixed rules (“if form submitted, send email”).
- **AI agent:** understands goals and unstructured input, decides on steps, uses tools and asks a person when unsure.

The real value appears when several agents work together in **one app**, each with a clear role.

## What agents can do, department by department

### Marketing
Plan campaigns, write and schedule social posts, create on-brand images, prepare newsletters and summarise campaign results.

### Content
Research topics and keywords, draft blog articles and landing pages, refresh outdated content and adapt texts for different channels — with an editor approving before publishing.

### SEO and GEO
Monitor rankings and AI-answer visibility, detect technical issues, and draft fixes and content updates.

### Sales
Qualify incoming leads, enrich them with company information, draft personalised follow-ups and keep the CRM up to date.

### Customer support
Answer common questions 24/7 from your knowledge base, collect details for complex cases and escalate to the right person.

### Analytics
Build weekly reports, spot anomalies in GA4 or advertising data and explain in plain language what changed.

### Operations
Process invoices, forms and documents, move data between systems and prepare summaries for approval.

## Where agents work best

Good candidates are tasks that are **frequent**, **text- or data-heavy**, **follow recognisable patterns** and **cost skilled people time**. Poor candidates are rare, high-risk decisions with no clear rules.

## How to start without risk

1. **Map the work** — list repetitive tasks and the time they take.
2. **Pick one department** with a clear, measurable win.
3. **Design guardrails** — permissions, approvals and logging.
4. **Pilot for a few weeks** and measure time saved and quality.
5. **Expand** to more agents and skills based on the results.
