**Short answer:** off-the-shelf property management software is the right choice when your processes match what it offers. Custom software pays off when you manage a specific portfolio mix, need deep integrations, want automation across your workflows or are spending more time on spreadsheet workarounds than on managing properties.

## What off-the-shelf tools do well

- Quick to start, predictable subscription cost
- Standard features: units, tenants, leases, rent tracking, maintenance tickets
- Regular updates handled by the vendor

## Where they start to hurt

- Processes adapted to the software instead of the other way round
- Missing integrations with accounting, banks or listing portals
- Limited reporting for owners and investors
- Per-unit pricing that grows with your portfolio
- Data spread across several tools and spreadsheets

## What custom software can add

- Workflows that match how you actually operate
- Owner and tenant portals with your branding
- Integrations with accounting, payments, e-signature and listing portals
- Automated reminders, renewals and document generation
- **AI assistance** for maintenance triage, tenant questions and reporting
- Dashboards across properties, owners and markets

## How to decide

1. List your core processes and the time each takes per month.
2. Mark where current tools force manual work or workarounds.
3. Estimate the cost of that manual work over three years.
4. Compare it with the cost of a custom platform (or a hybrid: standard tool plus custom integrations).

Often the best answer is a **phased approach**: start with the most painful workflow, integrate it with existing tools, and expand.
