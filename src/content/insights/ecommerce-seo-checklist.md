**Short answer:** e-commerce SEO wins on category pages first, then product pages. Give every important category unique content and clean URLs, control faceted navigation, add complete Product structured data, keep pages fast and link them logically — and make product information easy for AI shopping assistants to understand.

## Category pages

- A unique, helpful introduction (and buying advice further down the page)
- Clean, descriptive URLs and titles that match search terms
- Breadcrumbs with BreadcrumbList markup
- Links to related categories and top products

## Product pages

- Unique descriptions — never just the manufacturer text
- Specifications in a clear table
- High-quality images with descriptive alt text
- Product markup: price, currency, availability, brand, reviews where genuine
- FAQs that answer real pre-purchase questions

## Faceted navigation and duplicates

Filters can create thousands of URLs. Decide which combinations deserve indexing, use canonical tags for the rest and avoid linking crawlers into endless parameter combinations.

## Speed and Core Web Vitals

Compress and resize images (try our [image compressor](/tools/image-compressor/)), lazy-load below-the-fold media, limit third-party scripts and test on mobile.

## Out-of-stock and discontinued products

Keep temporarily unavailable products live with clear availability. For discontinued items, redirect to the closest alternative or category.

## Content that supports buying

Buying guides, comparisons and how-to articles capture early-stage searches and are frequently cited in AI answers.

## Tracking

Implement GA4 e-commerce events and Consent Mode v2 so you can connect SEO work to revenue.
